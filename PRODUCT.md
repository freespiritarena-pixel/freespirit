# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Canadian families and adult riders in equal measure. Parents are choosing a safe, credible place for children (ages 5+) to learn to ride; adults are beginners or returning riders looking for structured, respectful instruction. Both arrive evaluating trust: is this barn safe, is it serious, will my rider progress, and how do I start.

## Product Purpose

Free Spirit is a professional horse riding training academy in Canada. The website introduces the academy and converts interest into a booked trial lesson. Success: a visitor understands the programs, trusts the coaching and horse care, and submits the trial-lesson inquiry.

## Positioning

Horse welfare first, classical foundations, confidence through progression. The academy teaches riding the classical way, with the horse's wellbeing as the non-negotiable starting point, and moves each rider forward in deliberate stages (first lesson to first show).

## Operating Context

Year-round riding in a Canadian climate (indoor arena for winter, outdoor ring and trails in season). Programs: Beginner Lessons, Youth Program, Hunter/Jumper Training, Dressage Foundations, Summer Camps, Boarding and Training. Prices, when shown, are in CAD. Future English/French language support is expected.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Home page only for now; other routes are not built.
- Trial-lesson inquiry form posts to a stubbed `/api/inquiry` route; no backend or CRM is connected yet.
- Equestrian Canada membership is claimed by the brief.
- Undecided: real location, stats, coach names, testimonials, pricing, phone, email, social accounts.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: "Free Spirit" with a small horseshoe mark.
- Palette pinned by the owner: navy #030F21, gold #F7E3BE, ivory #FBFBF8, with role rules (gold never as text on ivory).
- Voice: confident, calm, welcoming; warm, trustworthy, premium; not a template, not a booking app.

## Evidence on Hand

No photography, video, testimonials, coach bios, or verified stats exist. All such content must be visibly marked placeholders (e.g. "[City, Province]", "[Coach name]") and must not be presented as real. One hero photo may be supplied later.

## Product Principles

1. The horse's welfare is shown, not just stated.
2. Trust before persuasion: calm clarity over hype.
3. Every rider has a visible next step.
4. Year-round seriousness: the academy works in every Canadian season.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast, keyboard-friendly navigation and form, visible focus states, prefers-reduced-motion respected, 44px tap targets. Future bilingual (EN/FR) audience.
